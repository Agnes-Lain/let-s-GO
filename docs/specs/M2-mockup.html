Let's GO — M2 board design mockup

The Go board used by every lesson and game, shown on the developer "Board demo" screen at 360×740 (中文, English, Français). Bamboo board, calm 山水 · 茶 · 竹 colors from the design tokens. Touch-down shows a ghost stone, lifting the finger plays it, sliding off cancels. Please approve before development. Spec: docs/specs/M2-board-ui.md.

Motion & sound

  • Stone lands: settles from 90 % to full size in 0.12 s, with one soft wooden "tock" (respects the silent switch; can be muted).
  • Capture: captured stones fade and shrink in 0.3 s. Illegal point: dashed ring fades in 0.7 s, no sound.
  • Hint ring: gentle amber pulse, 3 times, then still. "Reduce motion" turns every animation into an instant change.

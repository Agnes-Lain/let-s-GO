Let's GO — M3 lesson framework design mockup

The home map, the explanation player, the four exercise runners (tap-point, play-move, choice, count) with correct / wrong / hint / revealed feedback, and the result screen — all at 360×740, in 中文, English and Français. Calm 山水 · 茶 · 竹 colors from the design tokens; no leaves, no confetti, no red flashes. Positions are the sample lesson of the spec (§9), each checked by hand (spec §9.1). Please approve before development. Spec: docs/specs/M3-lesson-framework.md.

  • Teal fill ring: a selected point / a liberty (气 qì).
  • Amber pulsing ring: the hint (3 pulses, then static).
  • Green ring: the right answer (shown after 3 mistakes).
  • Dashed rouge ring: a mistake. Soft, no sound.
  • Dashed faint stone: a captured stone fading away (300 ms).

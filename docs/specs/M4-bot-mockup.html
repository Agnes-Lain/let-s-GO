Let's GO — M4 item 2 mockup: Capture Go (吃子棋) vs the bot

Unit 6 on the map, the game screen (your turn, bot thinking, atari warning, hint, illegal move), win, lose and lose-after-three-tries, and the result screen. Approved M3/M4 design at 360×740, 山水 · 茶 · 竹 colours from src/theme/tokens.ts; no leaves, no confetti. Positions are the hand-verified ones in docs/specs/M4-bot-capture-go.md (§8, §12); the mid-game and end positions follow one legal move sequence for game 3 (listed in each caption).

  • Slots under You / Bot: stones still needed; a filled slot is a captured stone (a white stone for Black's captures).
  • Teal border on a score half: whose turn it is. The pill below says it in words.
  • Small dot on a stone: the last move. Dashed faint stone: just captured, fading away.
  • Amber pulsing ring: hint. Green ring: the winning move. Dashed rouge ring: illegal tap or the move that hurt you (soft, no sound).
  • Static HTML and SVG; the three dots animate in CSS only (off with reduce-motion).

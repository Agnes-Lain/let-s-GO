Let's GO — M5 item 1 mockup (Ko, forbidden points, ladder)

Spec: docs/specs/M5-ko-forbidden-ladders.md. Almost everything in this item uses screens you already approved (M3/M4). This page shows the two things that are worth a look: A how a ko "closed point" appears (existing screens, new content only), and B the only genuinely new screen, the "play the whole ladder" runner, which exists only if you choose Option A in decision 1. If you choose Option B (the recommendation) section B is not built.

  • Rouge cross: a point nobody may play right now (used in explanation steps only). Triangle: the stone that was just played. Teal rings: liberties.
  • Dashed rouge ring: a mistake (soft, no sound). Calm 山水 · 茶 · 竹 colours from src/theme/tokens.ts; no leaves, no confetti.
  • The boards on this page are drawn by a small script for convenience; the app draws the same thing with react-native-svg.

A · Ko on the existing screens (no new UI)

Ko needs no new component. The explanation step marks the closed point with the existing cross; in an exercise, tapping the closed point shows the engine's ko sentence in the feedback card and costs nothing.

B · "Play the whole ladder" runner ONLY IF OPTION A

A new exercise type sequence (the light version needed for ladders). The learner plays Black's moves; White answers by itself after a short pause. Progress is shown as pips in the top bar instead of the usual segments only while a sequence is running. Everything else (prompt card, feedback card, hint, Try again) is the M3 frame.
